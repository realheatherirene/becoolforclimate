# BeCOOLforClimate
a prompt-based climate conversation tool for universal use

---

## 🎨 About the Project
**COOL** is a four‑point prompt sequence that helps people navigate climate conversations with confidence, clarity, and critical thinking.

**C-O-O-L** stands for: **Clarify** your actions, **Observe** your impacts, **Own** your responsibility, **Live** your values 

It’s a discussion guide designed to help both individuals and organizations communicate clearly, make meaningful choices, and push for the systemic changes needed to protect and beautify our home planet.

* **Creator:** Heather Succio
* **Status:** Open Source / Creative Commons

## 📥 Download Assets
You can find the following assets in this repository:
* **The pocket guide:** five playing-card size cards (2.5 × 3.5 inches), live at [becoolforclimate.com](https://becoolforclimate.com). Print them from the page, or download a PDF:
  * `/assets/COOL_Pocket_Guide_Cut_Sheet.pdf`: all five cards on one letter page, with cut lines.
  * `/assets/COOL_Pocket_Guide_Two_Sided.pdf`: the same page, plus a page of card backs for two-sided printing.
* **README:** Use guidelines.

Print at 100% scale, not "fit to page." Every word on the cards lives in `cool.js`; change it there and the page, the cards, and the print sheet all follow. The PDFs are made from the page's print view, so remake them after a change.
