#BeCOOLforClimate

a climate conversation tool

COOL is a four‑point prompt sequence that helps people navigate climate conversations with confidence, clarity, and critical thinking.

C-O-O-L stands for: Clarify your actions, Observe your impacts, Own your responsibility, and Live your values

It's a discussion map designed to help both individuals and organizations communicate clearly, make meaningful choices, and push for the systemic changes needed to protect and beautify our home planet.

Use the pocket guide

Each step asks two ways. Organizations use COOL to tell the truth about their impacts. Everyone else uses it to check those claims with open eyes.
